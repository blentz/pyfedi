## Task 1: `feed_list`'s three defects

**Pins**: another user's private feed title in the response body; a 500 from a
request with no arguments (assert the `TypeError`, since the test client
re-raises); the raw `<img src=x onerror=alert(1)>` in the body.

**Fixes**: the acting user from `current_user.id`; `request.args.get(name, 0,
type=int)` at all three reads, the idiom `show_feed:459` already uses; and
`markupsafe.escape` on the interpolated title.

**Inversions with controls**: the snooper sees *their own* feeds rather than
nothing (or the repair is indistinguishable from an empty response); a
no-argument request returns 200 with a usable body; the payload appears escaped
AND the surrounding markup still renders.
